# AND-107 Product Requirements

## Elevator Data Queries

The chatbot answers questions about specific elevators, inspections, and incidents using live data from the database. When a user asks about real data, the chatbot responds with accurate, current information.

### Required Queries (Minimum Five)

The operations team has identified five queries the chatbot must handle from day one:

1. "Which elevators have been shut down by TSSA?"
2. "Show me the inspection history for elevator [ID]"
3. "How many incidents were reported last year?"
4. "What incidents have been reported for elevator [ID]?"
5. "Which elevators have a 'Follow up' on their most recent inspection?"

### Additional Queries

Beyond the five required queries, at least three additional queries useful to the operations team must be identified and documented. For each, document what it is and why an operations team member would need it.

**Deliverable:** `docs/additional-queries.md`
